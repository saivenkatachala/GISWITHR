📌 How to Use
Click any lab section in the sidebar, expand exercise cards, then click ▶ View Output to see the output image. Place your JPEG screenshots in an images/ folder next to this file, named exactly as shown (e.g. images/ex-csv-read.jpeg). Use ◀ Prev / Next ▶ buttons at the bottom to navigate between sections.
